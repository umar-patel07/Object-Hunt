# Object Hunt: Hide in Plain Sight 🎭

## 📌 About the Game

Object Hunt: Hide in Plain Sight is a 3D multiplayer prop-hunt game inspired by traditional hide-and-seek.

Players are divided into two roles: the Seeker and the Hiders. Hiders can transform into everyday objects found in the environment and use disguises, strategy, and deception to avoid being discovered.

The Seeker must explore the environment, identify suspicious objects, and capture all Hiders before the round ends.

The game focuses on observation, creativity, strategy, and multiplayer fun.

---

## 🎮 Game Objective

The game includes two teams:

### 🔍 Seeker

The Seeker must:

- Search the environment for hidden players.
- Observe objects carefully.
- Identify disguised Hiders.
- Interact with suspicious objects.
- Capture all Hiders before the timer ends.

### 🪑 Hiders

Hiders must:

- Transform into objects in the environment.
- Find suitable hiding locations.
- Blend in with surrounding objects.
- Avoid being identified by the Seeker.
- Survive until the round timer expires.

---

## 👥 Multiplayer

The game supports local multiplayer for **2–5 players**.

| Total Players | Seeker | Hiders |
|--------------|--------|--------|
| 2            | 1      | 1      |
| 3            | 1      | 2      |
| 4            | 1      | 3      |
| 5            | 1      | 4      |

One player becomes the Seeker, while the remaining players become Hiders.

Roles can be rotated between rounds to give every player an opportunity to play as the Seeker.

## 🎭 Object Transformation System

Hiders can transform into suitable objects found in the environment.

### Transformation Mechanics

- Select an available object.
- Transform into the selected object.
- Match the object's appearance and size.
- Rotate the transformed object.
- Move carefully when necessary.
- Hide among similar objects.
- Return to the original player form when permitted.

### Example Transformations

- Chairs
- Tables
- Boxes
- Bottles
- Plants
- Lamps
- Books
- Buckets
- Crates
- Decorative objects

Transformed objects should blend naturally into their surroundings.

## ⏱️ Gameplay Flow

### 1. Main Menu

Players can:

- Start a game.
- View instructions.
- View controls.
- Open settings.
- Exit the game.

### 2. Player Setup

- Players join the game.
- The player count is confirmed.
- Controls are assigned.
- The Seeker is selected.
- Remaining players become Hiders.

### 3. Hiding Phase

- Hiders explore the environment.
- Hiders select objects and hiding positions.
- The Seeker remains restricted during the preparation phase.

### 4. Hunting Phase

- The Seeker enters the environment.
- The Seeker searches for Hiders.
- Hiders attempt to remain undetected.
- Hiders may reposition when allowed.

### 5. Round Ending

The round ends when:

- All Hiders are captured.
- The timer reaches zero.

### 6. Results Screen

The results screen displays:

- Winning team.
- Captured Hiders.
- Surviving Hiders.
- Round statistics.
- Restart option.
- Main menu option.

---

## 🏆 Winning Conditions

### Seeker Victory

The Seeker wins by capturing all Hiders before the timer expires.

### Hider Victory

The Hiders win when at least one Hider survives until the timer reaches zero.

The winning team must be displayed clearly at the end of the round.
